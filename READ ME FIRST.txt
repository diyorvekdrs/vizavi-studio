VIZAVI STUDIO - READ ME FIRST
=============================

Vizavi Studio makes videos together with Claude (claude.ai): you give it a voice recording (and music,
pictures, a script...), it prepares a package, Claude designs the video, and your PC renders it.


WHAT YOU NEED
- Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit
- Internet for the first start (it downloads 2-8 GB, depending on your PC)
- About 15 GB free disk space
- A claude.ai account
- A graphics card is NOT required. An NVIDIA card makes renders much faster; without one,
  everything runs on the processor and the app tells Claude to keep scenes lighter.


INSTALL (about 5-30 minutes, mostly waiting)
1. Right-click the zip -> "Extract All..." -> open the extracted folder.
2. Double-click "Install Vizavi Studio".
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
   If it asks for permission to install the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime: click "Yes".
3. The app opens and shows "Getting Vizavi Studio ready" while it installs what YOUR PC can use.
   Keep it open until the bottom bar says "Ready". Later starts are instant.
4. You can delete the downloaded zip and the extracted folder afterwards.

You'll find "Vizavi Studio" on the Desktop and in the Start menu.
Your videos are saved in your Videos folder -> "Vizavi Studio" (change it in Settings).


MAKING A VIDEO
1. Drop a voice recording (wav/mp3/m4a...) on the app window. Fill in "What I want"
   (and "Take care of" for must-haves), choose a preset, click "Create package for Claude".
2. Click "Copy message", open a NEW chat on claude.ai, drag the zip from the folder that opened
   into the chat, paste the message (Ctrl+V) and send.
3. When Claude replies with a zip, just download it. The app picks it up from your Downloads folder
   and renders a preview.
4. Watch the preview. Happy -> "Approve and render final". Changes wanted -> write notes, then drag
   the review zip into the SAME Claude chat with the copied message.
5. The final video appears in the project's output folder ("Open output folder").

The first transcription downloads the speech models once (a few GB) - that one takes longer.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- Settings (bottom left) -> "Check and repair components". Settings -> "This PC" shows what the
  app found on your computer and how it renders.
- After installing/updating an NVIDIA driver, press "Check and repair components" too.
- The log is at the bottom right of the app window.
- To update: run the newer "Install Vizavi Studio" over it. Your projects and settings stay.
- To remove: Windows Settings -> Apps -> "Vizavi Studio" -> Uninstall. Your videos stay.


GOOD TO KNOW
- For personal, non-commercial use: the precise word timing uses Meta's MMS model, licensed
  CC-BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial). You can turn it off in Settings.
- Everything runs on your own PC; the app only talks to Claude through the zips you drag in, and looks
  for new versions on GitHub (Settings -> Updates turns that off).

UPDATES
When a new version is out, the app shows "Vizavi Studio X is available" at the top.
Click "What's new" to read about it and "Update now" to install it: it takes a minute and the
window reconnects by itself. Your projects, settings and installed components are never touched.
If something is wrong after an update: Settings -> Updates -> "Undo last update".
